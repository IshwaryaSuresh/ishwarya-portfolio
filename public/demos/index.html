Three ways to make the homepage alive.

Each demo uses your real headline, palette, and project content. Try all three on desktop and on your phone, then pick the one that should become the homepage.

Demo A · scroll-driven

The Traveling Screen

A floating browser window carrying your real work travels down the page, docking beside each section and swapping screens as it goes. The closest translation of the yadom jar, but the object is your portfolio.
Demo B · scroll-driven

The Brand Object

A 3D teal monogram coin spins with your scroll and escorts the visitor through the page. More atmospheric, less literal. Keeps attention on the text.
Demo C · drag & throw

Sticker Physics

Your projects as loud draggable stickers scattered over the hero. Grab one, throw it, watch it bounce. Yadom's playful energy, contained to one memorable moment.
